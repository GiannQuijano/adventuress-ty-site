Kia Ora:A New Zealand Journey

Nine days and eight nights from Queenstown to Christchurch. Cruise Lake Wakatipu on the TSS Earnslaw, taste wine at Kinross, ride the Shotover Jet, cross the Southern Alps on the TranzAlpine train, and paddle a waka on the Avon River.

Eight nights of hotels, group airport transfers, guides, transportation and every listed experience are included, with 8 breakfasts, 2 lunches and a dinner.

Alpine presence

Five nights in Queenstown give you time to settle in: a gourmet BBQ lunch at Walter Peak High Country Farm, the Skyline Gondola and Stratosfare lunch, and an afternoon in Arrowtown.

Trip highlights:

  • TSS Earnslaw cruise and Walter Peak farm BBQ lunch
  • Classic Kinross Wine Experience
  • Skyline Gondola, luge rides and Stratosfare lunch
  • Kea 6-Line Ziptrek Ecotour
  • Shotover Jet and Oxbow off-roader
  • TranzAlpine train across the Southern Alps
  • Waka paddle and Kai Safari food tour in Christchurch
