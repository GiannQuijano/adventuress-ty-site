Costa Rica Escape

February 11–17, 2027 Arenal Observatory Lodge · Fonda Vela · Tamarindo Diria Book your trip

Pura Vida Luxe:A Costa Rica Escape

Seven days and six nights across Arenal, Monteverde and Tamarindo, curated for the traveler who wants wonder without the work. Waterfalls and hot springs by the volcano, the cloud forest canopy, and a slow finish on the Pacific coast.

Private transportation, transfers and a professional guide are arranged before you land in Liberia. You simply arrive, exhale and savor it, alongside a small circle of travelers who will feel like old friends by the farewell dinner.

Rainforest presence

Your first two nights are at Arenal Observatory Lodge, on the volcano’s shoulder. Day one ends with a 530-step walk to La Fortuna Waterfall for a swim, then dinner at EcoTermales Hot Springs.

Trip highlights:

  • La Fortuna Waterfall swim and EcoTermales Hot Springs dinner
  • Waterfall rappelling, canyoning and a zipline across the canyon
  • White-water rafting on the Balsa River
  • ATV ride around Arenal Volcano and the Maleku Indigenous Village
  • Boat across Lake Arenal to Monteverde
  • Extreme canopy zipline, Tarzan swing and a night wildlife walk
  • Coffee, chocolate and sugar cane tour, then two nights in Tamarindo
