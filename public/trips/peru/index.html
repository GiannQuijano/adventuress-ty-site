Peru Adventure

April 25 – May 1, 2028 Sacred Valley · Starlodge · Aguas Calientes · Cusco Book your trip

Into the Andes:A Journey Through Peru

Seven days and six nights from the Sacred Valley to Machu Picchu and Cusco. Visit the Maras salt mines and Moray terraces, sleep in a transparent capsule on a cliff at Starlodge, then ride the scenic train through the Andes to Machu Picchu.

Six nights of hotels, private transportation, an English-speaking guide, the train to and from Machu Picchu and the domestic flight from Lima to Cusco are included.

Andean presence

On day three, choose a zipline or the Via Ferrata to reach your Starlodge capsule, with an open-air hot tub and dinner. The next morning you descend and ride the train to Aguas Calientes.

Trip highlights:

  • Private Sacred Valley tour: Maras salt mines and Moray
  • Misminay community music, dance and textiles
  • A night in a Starlodge capsule with an open-air hot tub
  • Zipline or Via Ferrata at Pachar
  • Scenic train through the Andes
  • Guided visit to Machu Picchu
  • Cusco city tour: Qoricancha, Sacsayhuamán and the cathedral
