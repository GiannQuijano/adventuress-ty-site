Jenesis House Retreat

April 1–4, 2027 Sedona, Arizona · Wellness retreat Book your trip

Breathe Deep:A Sedona Wellness Retreat

Four days in Sedona’s red rock country, made for rest. Mornings begin with yoga or Pilates, afternoons bring a massage and a facial, and evenings settle into a floating sound bath and a fireside Let It Go ceremony.

Your accommodations, nourishing meals and local transportation are all arranged. This is a land-only retreat: bring yourself, and leave the planning to us.

Wellness presence

Every day includes nourishing meals and snacks and a morning movement class. Your stay includes a 60-minute massage, a 60-minute facial, and an optional hike at your own pace through the red rocks.

Trip highlights:

  • 60-minute facial
  • Daily morning movement: yoga and Pilates
  • Floating sound bath
  • Fireside Let It Go ceremony
  • Nourishing meals and snacks every day
