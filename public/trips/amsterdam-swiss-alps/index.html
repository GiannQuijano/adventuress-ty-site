Amsterdam to the Alps

May 23 – June 3, 2028 Amsterdam · Cologne · Frankfurt · Lucerne · Interlaken · Zurich Book your trip

Grand Tour:Amsterdam to the Swiss Alps

Twelve days and eleven nights through the Netherlands, Germany and Switzerland. A sunset dinner cruise on Amsterdam’s canals, first-class trains along the Rhine, fondue in Lucerne, the First Flyer zipline above Grindelwald and a sunset cruise on Lake Thun.

Eleven nights of hotels, group arrival and departure transfers, private coaches, first-class rail where listed and every included experience are arranged, with 11 breakfasts, a lunch and four dinners.

Alpine presence

Two nights in Lucerne and two in Interlaken bring the Alps close: a group dinner of fondue or raclette, a gondola to Grindelwald-First, and an evening sunset cruise on Lake Thun.

Trip highlights:

  • A’DAM LOOKOUT rooftop swing and Amsterdam sunset dinner cruise
  • First-class train from Amsterdam to Cologne
  • Calmont Klettersteig, Europe’s steepest vineyard climb
  • Segway Green Tour and group lunch in Frankfurt
  • Swiss fondue or raclette dinner in Lucerne
  • First Flyer zipline at Grindelwald-First
  • Lake Thun sunset dinner cruise
