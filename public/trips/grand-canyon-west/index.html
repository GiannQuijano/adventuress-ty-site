Grand Canyon West

September 9–11, 2027 Luxury Stargazer Domes · VIP all-access Book your trip

Beyond Ordinary:A Grand Canyon VIP Escape

Three days and two nights at Grand Canyon West with VIP all-access. See the canyon from a helicopter, on horseback, by ATV, from the Skywalk and on a canopy zipline, then end the day under the stars.

Two nights in a luxury Stargazer Dome, gourmet meals, snacks and unlimited beverages are included. Drive or fly in, and the rest is arranged.

Canyon presence

Day two is the full experience: helicopter, horseback, ATV, the Skywalk and the canopy zipline, plus a Hualapai Indigenous performance. Evenings close with stargazing and a campfire.

Trip highlights:

  • Helicopter experience
  • Grand Canyon West All-Access Pass and the Skywalk
  • Horseback and ATV riding
  • Canopy zipline
  • Hualapai Indigenous performance
  • Evening stargazing and campfire
  • Guano Point and Eagle Point
