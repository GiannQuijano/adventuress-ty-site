Mediterranean Cruise

August 20–29, 2027 Norwegian Epic · Balcony stateroom · From Barcelona Book your trip

La Dolce Vita:A Mediterranean Sailing

Seven days at sea from Barcelona aboard the Norwegian Epic, calling at Provence, Nice and Monaco, Florence and Pisa, Sicily, Salerno and Rome. You wake in a new port, and every evening ends on your own balcony.

Round-trip airfare from your home airport, a night in Barcelona and every transfer are included. Onboard, the open bar, dining, Wi-Fi and gratuities are already taken care of.

Coastal presence

Your balcony stateroom is your private window on the Mediterranean. Between ports there is a day at sea for the spa, the pool deck or a long lunch, plus three specialty dining meals.

Trip highlights:

  • Balcony stateroom aboard the Norwegian Epic
  • Ports: Provence, Nice and Monaco, Florence and Pisa
  • Messina in Sicily, Salerno and Rome
  • Unlimited open bar
  • Three specialty dining meals
  • $100 onboard credit and $50 excursion credit per room
  • A day at sea to unwind
