About us

Our story

Who is Adventuress Ty?

Adventuress Ty is a curated group-travel collective founded by Ty Vaughn for women who want to see the world beautifully and together. From candlelit dinners in Costa Rica to sunsets over Santorini, every journey is planned down to the last detail, so all you do is arrive, exhale and savor it.

Join the crew
Seamlessly planned, effortlessly enjoyed.

We take care of every detail, so the only thing you have to plan is what to wear to dinner.

Small groups, beautiful hotels and an itinerary that leaves room to breathe. Our travelers come for the destination and stay for the company.

Why travel with Adventuress Ty?

  • Every detail arranged: boutique hotels, private transfers, guides and reservations.
  • A warm, welcoming circle of travelers. Come solo and leave with sisters.
  • Clear pricing, a simple deposit and flexible payment plans.
  • Ty and her team travel with you, from the first toast to the last goodbye.

Our mission

About the founder

Meet Ty Vaughn

A visionary in curated travel

Ty Vaughn didn’t grow up traveling. When she discovered it, it felt like an awakening, and she began bringing friends along. What started as a few friends on a weekend away has grown into a community that sails the Mediterranean, dines under the stars and takes in the world’s most breathtaking views together.

Ty personally curates every itinerary and travels with each group. Her philosophy is simple: “If it’s not an adventure, then it’s not worth it.” Her team, Jerusha Day, Mikaela Davis and Ashley Hopson, makes sure every moment is captured and every guest is cared for.
