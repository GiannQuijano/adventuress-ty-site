Policies

Terms + conditions

  • All sales are final. Payments are non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • Registering holds your spot for 24 hours. Your spot is confirmed once payment is received.
  • Each trip page lists exactly what is and isn’t included, along with the deposit and the final payment date.

Payment policies

  • Most journeys are reserved with a $520 deposit per person, which includes a $20 processing fee. Shorter getaways start at $312.
  • After your deposit you can pay the balance in monthly installments or set up auto pay.
  • Need more time? Send a payment extension request before your due date.
  • Ready to pay? Make a payment.

Travel insurance

Travel insurance is highly recommended for every traveler. Because all payments are final, coverage protects your investment if plans change. Ask us which plans past travelers have used.

Privacy policy

Adventuress Ty uses the details you share with us only to plan your trip and keep you informed about your reservation. Questions about your information? Email info@adventuressty.com.
